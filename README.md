# Narendra Thapa's Tech Comparison Portal

## Overview

This portal is a personal, dynamic website built to showcase my professional profile, expertise, and provide unbiased comparisons of leading technologies in the IT industry.  
It is designed for tech professionals, recruiters, and enthusiasts who want to quickly understand the strengths and weaknesses of top tools in AI, Cloud, Security, Monitoring, and DevOps.

## What I Want To Do

- **Present my professional brand:** Highlight my experience, skills, and achievements in AI, cloud, automation, and infrastructure.
- **Share knowledge:** Offer clear, up-to-date comparisons of major technology platforms and tools.
- **Help others choose the right tech:** Make it easy for visitors to compare solutions and make informed decisions.
- **Stay current:** Regularly update the portal with new trends, tools, and industry news.
- **Connect:** Provide easy access to my LinkedIn, email, and other contact options for networking and collaboration.

## End Goal

- **Become a go-to resource** for technology comparisons and insights in the IT community.
- **Showcase my expertise** and attract professional opportunities (consulting, speaking, collaboration).
- **Empower visitors** to make better technology choices for their projects and organizations.
- **Demonstrate my skills** in building scalable, dynamic web solutions using modern web technologies and automation.

## Features

- **Dynamic homepage:** Personal bio, skills, and links to comparison pages.
- **Comparison pages:** AI Tools, Cloud Providers, Security Platforms, Monitoring Solutions, DevOps Tools.
- **Latest tech trends:** Curated news and updates from the industry.
- **Easy to update:** All comparison data is stored in JSON files for quick editing.
- **Responsive design:** Works on desktop and mobile.

## How To Use

1. Browse the homepage to learn about my background and expertise.
2. Click on any comparison link to view detailed, unbiased tool comparisons.
3. Use the contact links to connect or collaborate.
