About Me

A Director of Engineering with 20+ years of experience transforming teams and technology. From hardware engineer to AI leader, I build resilient systems and empower people to do their best work. Passionate about cloud architecture, AI adoption, and engineering excellence.

Beyond the Code

With over two decades in the technology landscape, I've evolved from a hands-on hardware engineer to a Director of Engineering leading AI transformations. My career isn't just about mastering the latest tech stack—it's about building resilient systems and even more resilient teams. I believe that true engineering excellence happens at the intersection of technical innovation and human empathy.

I lead with a 'people-first, mission-always' philosophy. Whether it's migrating complex legacy systems to the cloud or integrating Generative AI into daily workflows, my focus is on empowering engineers to do their best work. I champion 'Vibe Coding'—an AI-augmented development approach where engineers leverage tools like GitHub Copilot, Claude, and Cursor as thinking partners. Instead of writing every line, teams focus on intent and architecture while AI handles boilerplate and suggestions. The result? 50% faster time-to-market, 75% shorter PR cycles, and 89% improvement in MTTR. Vibe Coding combines technical velocity with creative problem-solving, allowing us to tackle bigger challenges and ship better products faster.

Born into an Army family, adaptability is in my DNA. Moving across India—from Shimla to Shillong—taught me to embrace change as the only constant. This background shapes my leadership style: disciplined yet flexible, strategic yet tactical. When I'm not architecting cloud solutions, I'm exploring the frontiers of AI, mentoring the next generation of leaders, or simply enjoying the journey of continuous learning.
