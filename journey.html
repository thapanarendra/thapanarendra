My Journey

From Army kid to Director of Engineering - A story of discipline, adaptation, and continuous learning

1980s-1990s

🏕️ Army Kid Foundation

Born in Mhow Army Cantonment, Madhya Pradesh, to a Gorkha Rifles Army personnel with roots in Nepal. An Army upbringing across Shimla, Shillong, Indore, and Dehradun instilled discipline, adaptability, and resilience.

🎯 Character Building: Constant relocations taught me to adapt quickly, make friends easily, and embrace change as an opportunity for growth. The discipline of army life and witnessing my father's dedication to the nation shaped my core principles that guide me even today.

Late 1990s

💻 The Digital Awakening

Completed Bachelor of Commerce from Shillong during the dawn of the internet era. In my final year, I discovered computers and programming languages.

🔥 The Spark: Learning C programming and discovering the magic of making machines think ignited a passion that would define my entire career path. Joining NIIT wasn't just education - it was stepping into a future I could barely imagine.

2002-2004

🎯 The Jetking Strategic Choice

A pivotal moment at Jetking Institute in Indore. Their famous billboard contrasting software vs. hardware/networking professionals convinced me to pursue Hardware and Networking.

💡 Strategic Decision: Chose the road less traveled in India - Hardware and Networking over software development, which proved to be my competitive advantage.

2005

🔧 First Steps in Real World

My debut as Hardware and Network Engineer at Networking Information Services, managing IT infrastructure for manufacturing industries in Indore.

🎓 Hands-on University: Learning to troubleshoot servers at 2 AM, managing network outages, understanding that technology serves business - not the other way around.

2005-2007

🚀 The Pune Adventure

Moving to Pune - India's major IT Hub was both thrilling and terrifying. Pursued Red Hat Enterprise Linux certification as open-source was gaining momentum.

🔮 Technology Foresight: Betting on Linux when Windows dominated enterprise was a calculated risk that opened doors to the software industry. Landing at Symantec (Veritas) marked my transition to software professional.

2007-2016

🏛️ The Veritas Excellence Decade

10 transformative years at Veritas Technologies - from Associate to Principal Software Engineer. Specialized in Unix products (AIX, HP-UX), Veritas Cluster Server, and storage solutions.

🎯 Leadership Evolution: Learned SDLC, mastered engineering quality and reliability practices, developed leadership skills, and built relationships that last a lifetime. Veritas was my technology university.

2016-2018

☁️ The Cloud Revolution Era

Joined GS Lab (now Neurealm) as the cloud revolution was taking off. Led the transformation from traditional infrastructure to cloud-native architectures.

🌟 Cloud Pioneer: Mastering Azure, building cloud engineering practices, and establishing strategic partnerships with Microsoft. The shift from on-prem to cloud was the biggest technology transformation of my career.

2018-2021

🌩️ Multi-Cloud Mastery

Expanded expertise across Azure, AWS, and GCP. Led complex multi-cloud migrations and modernization projects for global customers.

🚀 Scaling Impact: Built strategic partnerships with cloud vendors, scaled engineering teams, and delivered enterprise-grade solutions that transformed how businesses operate.

2021-Present

🤖 The AI Product Renaissance

Leading the AI transformation as Director of Engineering. Pioneered AI adoption with GitHub Copilot, driving 50% productivity gains.

🎯 Future Forward: Building the next generation of AI-powered products, scaling teams, and fostering a culture of innovation. The AI revolution is just beginning, and we're at the forefront.

2024

🔄 The AI Methodology Era

A critical inflection point - moved from adopting AI tools to systematizing AI-driven engineering. Created Spec Coding methodology and formalized 'Vibe Coding' philosophy across the organization. Built frameworks like AIMI (AI Maturity Index) that transformed engineering velocity.

💡 Key Learning: Realized that 100% tool adoption wasn't enough - we needed a methodology. Built frameworks that transformed how 100+ engineers approach problem-solving daily, delivering +133% sprint velocity and 50% faster time-to-market organization-wide.

2026

🚀 Enterprise AI Leadership

Today - managing $7.5M portfolio, leading 100+ engineers across 3 continents (India, US, Europe), pioneering enterprise AI transformation at scale. From scaling teams 5x to building an AI-native organizational culture with 35% YoY growth and 4.7/5 customer satisfaction.

🎯 The Full Circle: The journey from that first hardware engineer role to leading an AI-first organization validates everything - adaptability from Army kid days, continuous learning from every role, and courage to bet on emerging technologies. The best is yet to come.
